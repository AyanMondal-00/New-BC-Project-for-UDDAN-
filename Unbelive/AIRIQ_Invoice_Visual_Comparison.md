# AIRIQ invoice visual comparison — Phase 1

## Scope and evidence

Visually inspected every page using Windows.Data.Pdf rasterization and image viewing: `AIRIQ Dummy Invoice.pdf` pages 1–2 and `AIRIQ_E-Invoice_103002_SAMPLE (1).pdf` page 1. This comparison uses the rendered appearance, supplemented by inspection of `AIRIQDummyInvoice.rdl` and Report 50390 AL. Temporary page images were written outside the project for viewing. Only this comparison document was created; no implementation files were changed and nothing was published.

Both PDFs report approximately 793.701 × 1122.520 Windows display units, equivalent to A4 210 × 297 mm. Measurements below are approximate visual measurements unless explicitly identified as current RDL properties. Different passenger counts and populated fields affect visual density; they are not data defects assessed here.

## Page-by-page findings

- **Generated page 1:** branding, invoice panels, two passenger rows, large blank interval, four GST detail rows, summary, narration, bank/contact and declaration/signature fit on the page. The bottom legal footer is absent.
- **Generated page 2:** almost entirely blank; only the horizontal footer rule/coral accent and legal footer appear near its top. No repeated invoice header appears.
- **Client page 1:** all sections, five passenger rows, two tax rows, declaration/signature and legal footer form one balanced page. The footer rule is near the bottom edge. This is a sample with a watermark and sample warning, not a production content template.

## Differences intentionally excluded from implementation proposals

The target shows a diagonal SAMPLE watermark and red sample warning; these should not be copied into a production invoice. It also has populated e-invoice values, a visible QR, different dates, formatted currency, passenger serial values, GST summary lines, amount-in-words, bank/contact content and page numbering. These observations do not authorize changing mappings, expressions, tax presentation, serial logic, QR functionality or business logic. The current tax explanatory text differs from the sample; preserve its wording in this formatting phase.

## Prioritized implementation sequence for approval

1. Fix dynamic vertical flow and footer spill; preserve any-number passenger/GST detail behavior. Validate both short and longer invoices after implementation.
2. Match passenger table header color/contrast and simplify table grid styling.
3. Match panel corner treatment using a renderer-compatible technique, without changing dataset expressions or introducing sample QR content.
4. Normalize heading typography, internal padding, reference alignment and signature alignment.
5. Refine header position, summary/total bar proportions and lower panel spacing as one coordinated A4 composition.

This is a comparison and proposal only. No compilation or implementation testing was necessary because AL/RDL were not modified. Phase 2 requires approval before applying changes. Exact final positions must be confirmed through newly rendered Business Central PDFs; static RDL coordinates alone cannot guarantee pagination after dynamic tablix growth.
